How we work

Coaching, not consulting

We don't do your analysis for you, and we're not a recruiter or certification body. The point is that you can write the query, defend the number and explain it to someone who doesn't care how clever it was.

One rule matters in this field: don't bring confidential, personal or regulated data into a coaching session. We work on anonymized samples, public datasets or your own structures described in general terms, and your employer's data policy always comes first.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need to know Python?

Not for most analysis work. SQL plus a BI tool covers a great deal, and we'll tell you honestly when a script would save you time.

I only know spreadsheets.

That's a fine starting point, and the concepts transfer. We start with SQL fundamentals and move at your pace.

Can I bring my company's data?

No. Bring the shape of the problem, anonymized samples, or a public dataset. Confidential data stays where it belongs.

Will this get me an analyst job?

It builds the skills those roles test. We're not recruiters, we issue no certificate, and we don't promise outcomes.

My dashboards get ignored.

Very common, and usually a framing problem rather than a design one. That's the stakeholders program.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
